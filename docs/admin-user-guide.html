Getting Started

Logging in & the Dashboard

The admin area is where your office staff manage everything. Employees have their own separate portal (covered where relevant).

  1. Open the admin web address and enter your email and password.
  2. You land on the Dashboard — a quick overview split into two groups of cards:
    • Operations — Cesspool, Septic, and Wastewater totals.
    • HR Portal — Employees, Documents, Announcements, Incident Reports, Community Calendar (each shows a quick count).
  3. Click any card to jump straight into that module. Below the cards, Quick Actions let you log a disposal or generate the monthly report.
Good to know
What you see depends on your permissions. If a menu item or card is missing, your role may not have access — an owner can grant it under User Management (module 12).

Finding your way around

  • The left sidebar is your main menu. The bell icon (top-right) shows notifications; the profile menu has logout.
  • Most list screens have a search box, filters (date range, status), and action buttons (Edit, Delete, Export).
  • Green "success" messages appear briefly at the top-right after you save something.

Part A — Operations (Field Records)

1. Waste Disposal (Disposal Details)

What it's for
Keeps a record of every waste pickup & haul, and produces the official County of Hawai'i monthly report.
Where: Sidebar → Disposal Details. New records are created by the public intake form (drivers fill it in the field); the office reviews them here.
How it works
  1. A driver/field user fills the public Log Waste Disposal form — date, generator, waste type, address, volume, discharge site, vehicle, etc.
  2. The record appears automatically in the Disposal Details list (and admins get a bell notification).
  3. Use the date / year filters to narrow the list.
  4. To export: tick the rows you want and click Export PDF (one record = one PDF; several = a zip), or Export CSV for a spreadsheet.
  5. For the County report: click Generate Monthly Report (also on the Dashboard), pick the month/year, fill the permit/registration/signature fields, and download the official PDF.
To make changes
Records come from the field form, so the office mainly reviews and exports. You can delete a record (it's kept recoverable, not erased). To change what the field form asks, that's a developer change to the public form.

2. Cesspool Records

What it's for
Stores cesspool inspection reports submitted from the field, with photos, video and the inspector's signature.
Where: Sidebar → Cesspool Records.
How it works
  1. An inspector fills the public Cesspool form in the field. They can save a draft (kept for 1 hour) and finish later.
  2. Submitted reports show in the list; filter by date or draft/submitted status.
  3. Click Edit to review a report, add or replace the photo/video/signature, and correct details.
  4. Click PDF to download the inspection report, or Send Report to email the PDF to one or more recipients.
To make changes
Open a record → Edit → change fields or swap media → Save. Deleting a record keeps it recoverable; the uploaded files stay on the server.

3. Septic Records

What it's for
Same as Cesspool Records, but for septic-tank inspections.
Where: Sidebar → Septic Records.

It works exactly like Cesspool Records: field form (with draft save) → review/edit in the office → PDF download or email. The only difference is the septic email report goes to a single recipient (cesspool allows several).

Part B — HR Portal

4. Employees

What it's for
Creates and manages the login accounts your crew use for the Employee Portal (documents, announcements, incident reports, their PTO, etc.).
Where: Sidebar → HR Portal → Employees.
How to add an employee
  1. Click + New Employee.
  2. Enter their name, email, and a password, and set the account Active.
  3. Save. The system automatically emails them a welcome message with their login details.
How to edit an employee
  1. Click Edit on their row. Here you can update name, email, active status, Start Date, Accrues PTO (eligibility), and reset their password.
  2. The edit page also shows their Quarterly Assessment status, their PTO balance & history, and any documents they've signed.
Welcome email
If the welcome email didn't send (e.g. a mail hiccup), the edit page shows "Welcome email pending" — just set a new password and save to send it again. It's only ever sent once successfully.

5. Quarterly Assessments (90-day check-ins)

What it's for
Reminds you when each employee is due for their 90-day review, and keeps a record of completed assessments.
Where: On each employee's Edit page, and the PTO/Assessment columns on the Employees list.
How it works
  1. Set the employee's Start Date (their hire date) and save.
  2. The system counts 90 days and works out the next-due date automatically.
  3. When someone becomes due, all admins are notified on the dashboard bell and by email (a daily check runs in the background).
  4. After you do the review, open their edit page and click Mark Completed (add a note if you like). The next one is then scheduled 90 days later.
To make changes
Change the Start Date to shift the schedule. The Employees list shows each person as Due or On track at a glance, with their last-completed date.

6. PTO (Paid Time Off)

What it's for
A simple, days-based view of each employee's PTO for the year — so the crew can see what they have left. Your payroll stays the official record; this is just an easy reference that always counts down (no scary negative numbers).
Where: On each employee's Edit page (full management); a quick PTO column on the Employees list; and the employee's own Dashboard in their portal.
How to set it up for a person (once a year)
  1. Open the employee's Edit page and make sure Accrues PTO is on.
  2. In the PTO section, click Add PTO entry → choose Annual allotment → enter their days for the year (e.g. 27, 16, or 11) → Save.
  3. If they carried days from last year, add another entry → Carried over (up to 5 days).
How to log time off
  1. Click Add PTO entry → choose PTO used.
  2. Enter the number of days (use 0.5 for a half day) and the date they took off, then Save.
  3. The Remaining number updates instantly, and the employee sees it on their own dashboard with the dates listed.
To make changes
Use Adjustment (with + or −) to correct a balance. Every entry is listed with its date and who entered it, so nothing is hidden. Up to 5 days may carry to next year — you add that as a "Carried over" entry in January.
Not eligible?
Turn off Accrues PTO for anyone who doesn't get PTO (temporary/part-time). Their portal simply shows "not currently enrolled in PTO."

7. Documents (folders & read-and-sign)

What it's for
Shares company documents with staff — either openly, or assigned to specific people, with an optional mandatory "read & sign" that records a signed certificate.
Where: Sidebar → HR Portal → Documents (and Document Categories for the folders).
How to share a document
  1. First make a folder (Document Categories → add, e.g. "Policies", "Safety").
  2. Go to Documents → add a document, give it a title, pick the folder, and upload the file.
  3. Choose who sees it: Public (everyone) or assign it to specific employees.
  4. (Optional) For a PDF assigned to people, tick require read & sign and set a due date.
What the employee does
  1. In their portal's Document Library, they open the document. If it needs signing, they must read it and type their name to sign before they can download it.
  2. Signing creates a dated certificate (with their name, time, and IP) that you can download.
To track & change
The Signed Documents / acknowledgments view shows who has signed and who's still pending. Replace a file by editing the document; a folder can't be deleted while it still has documents in it.

8. Announcements

What it's for
Posts company news/updates that employees see in their portal.
Where: Sidebar → HR Portal → Announcements.
  1. Click add, give it a title and message, optionally an image, set it Active, and a publish date.
  2. Save — it immediately appears on the employee portal (the 3 latest also show on their dashboard).
To make changes
Edit to update the text/image, or turn Active off to hide it without deleting. Deleting removes it from the portal.

9. Community Calendar

What it's for
A shared calendar of company events (holidays, meetings, training, PTO, etc.) employees can view.
Where: Sidebar → HR Portal → Community Calendar.
  1. Click a day or "add event", enter a title, date(s), pick a category (each has its own colour), add a location, and optionally attach a flyer.
  2. Save — it shows on the calendar for staff and on the employees' portal calendar.
To make changes
Click an event to edit or delete it. Categories/colours are fixed (Holiday, Meeting, Training, PTO, Other).

10. Incident Reports

What it's for
Records workplace incidents — filed by employees from their portal, or by a manager on their behalf — and tracks each through to closed.
Where: Sidebar → HR Portal → Incident Reports.
How it works
  1. An employee submits a report from their portal (date, location, category, description, and up to 6 photos + 4 videos). Managers are notified.
  2. Each report gets a reference number automatically (e.g. IR-2026-0008).
  3. Managers open it, review, set the status (Open → Under Review → Closed), and add review notes. The affected employee is notified of status changes.
  4. Use the filters + Export CSV for reporting.
To make changes
Only managers (with the right permission) can edit/close reports. Deleting keeps the record recoverable and keeps the photos.

11. Google Reviews

What it's for
Shows your company's Google reviews inside the employee portal, so the crew can see what customers are saying.
Where: Employee portal → Reviews page (rating summary + review cards).
Status

The page is built and currently shows sample reviews so you can see the layout. It switches to your real, live Google reviews once your Google Business Profile is connected (this needs you to grant access and Google to approve it — a one-time setup).

To go live
Add our team as a Manager on your Google Business listing and allow the one-time setup. After that, reviews update automatically every day — nothing for you to maintain.

Part C — Administration

12. User Management (Roles, Users, Permissions)

What it's for
Controls who can log into the admin area and what they're allowed to do. (This is for office/staff accounts — employees are managed separately under Employees.)
Where: Sidebar → User Management → Roles, Users, Permissions.
The three pieces
  • Roles — job types like "Manager" or "Office Staff". Create a role, mark it active.
  • Permissions — a grid where you tick, per role, exactly what that role can see and do (view/create/edit/delete for each module).
  • Users — the actual staff logins. Create a user, set a password, and assign them a role.
How to give someone access
  1. Make sure a suitable Role exists (e.g. "Manager").
  2. In Permissions, open that role and tick what it may do, then save.
  3. In Users, create the person's login and assign them that role.
Safety rules built in
The Superadmin role always has full access and is protected (can't be deleted or locked out). You can't delete your own account or the last superadmin. A deactivated user (or role) immediately loses access.

13. Email Settings

What it's for
Sets which email addresses receive the monthly report reminder (the nudge to generate the County disposal report).
Where: Sidebar → Email Setting.
  1. Add/edit the recipient addresses (a default plus up to three more).
  2. On the last day of each month (9 AM Hawai'i time), the system emails those addresses a reminder automatically.
To make changes
Edit the record to change the recipients. (For the reminder to actually send, the server's scheduled task must be running — your developer/host sets that up once.)

14. Activity Log

What it's for
An automatic record of who did what — created, edited, deleted — across the system. Great for accountability and tracing a change.
Where: Sidebar → Activity Log (split into Forms and HR views).
  1. Open the log and use the filters (module, action, person, text, date range) to find what you're looking for.
  2. Click a row for the details of that change.
Good to know
The log is written automatically — there's nothing to maintain. It records the person, time, and what changed.

15. Notifications

What it's for
Keeps you informed in real time — new field submissions, incident reports, document sign-offs, and assessment-due reminders.
Where: The bell icon (top-right of every page) and the full Notifications page.
  1. A red number on the bell shows unread notifications; a pop-up also appears when something new arrives.
  2. Click a notification to jump straight to the item, or use Mark all read.
Good to know
Notifications are generated automatically by the system — no setup needed. Employees have their own bell in their portal too.
